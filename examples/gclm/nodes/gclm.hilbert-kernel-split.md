## Statement

For odd $f \in C^1(\mathbb{S}^1)$ and $\delta \in (0,\pi/2]$, the principal-value integral defining $Hf(0)$ splits into an inner contribution over $|y| \le \delta$ and an outer contribution bounded by $\frac{2}{\pi}\|f\|_{L^\infty}\log\csc(\delta/2)$.
